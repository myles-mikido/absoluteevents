Our Story

Events That Leave a
Lasting Impression

Absolute Events Limited, is an event management company in Kenya specializing in creating unforgettable event experiences tailored to meet the specific needs of both corporate and private clients. We offer end-to-end event solutions, handling everything from ideation and planning to production and on-the-day management.

Our commitment to excellence, innovation, and sustainability has earned us a reputation as a trusted partner in Kenya’s event industry.

Core Values: sustainability is not an afterthought. It is our edge. Zero waste logistics. Carbon neutral planning. Client-Centric Focus. Professionalism. The future of events, built today.

Elizabeth Githinji

CEO & Founder — Absolute Events Ltd

What We Offer

Services Built for
Bold Experiences

From intimate Brand Activations to city wide festivals, we engineer every event with precision, creativity, and a sustainability first mindset. Our holistic approach ensures that every event we manage is delivered on time, within budget, and to the highest professional standards.

Our Expertise

Corporate Events

We handle a broad range of corporate gatherings, including conferences, workshops, seminars, AGMs, product launches, gala dinners, team-building events, and corporate parties. Each event is carefully crafted to align with the company’s brand and objectives.

Conferences · Launches

Exhibition Installations & Management

We design and install customized exhibition stands and booths for trade shows, expos, and industry-specific exhibitions. Additionally, we handle the logistical coordination to ensure a smooth experience for exhibitors and visitors.

Exhibitions · Fairs
️

Food & Lifestyle Festivals

Organizers of the acclaimed Nairobi Food Festival we bring communities together through culture, cuisine, and curated entertainment.

Festivals · Food Events

Brand Activations

We create interactive brand experiences designed to engage customers and boost brand recognition. From in-store promotions to large-scale public activations, we ensure that brands connect meaningfully with their audience.

Activations · Campaigns

Event Equipment Rentals

We provide a wide range of event equipment for rent, including marquees, tents, chairs, tables and so many more. Our rentals guarantee top-quality, well-maintained items for every occasion.

Event Equipment · Rentals

Event Concepts & Creative Solutions

Our creative team works closely with clients to develop unique event concepts that align with their brand and purpose. From themed events to experiential activations, we bring the concept to life with our innovative solutions.

Event Concepts · Creative Solutions

Why Choose
Us ?

Experienced Team

Our dedicated team of event professionals has a combined experience of over 10 years in event management. We are skilled in delivering complex, high-profile events with precision and creativity.

Customized Services

We work closely with each client to understand their unique needs, ensuring that every event is customized to achieve their objectives and reflect their brand.

End-to-End Solutions

From ideation to execution, we provide a one-stop solution for all event management needs, allowing our clients to focus on enjoying their events without the stress of planning.

Focus on Sustainability

We integrate sustainable practices into our events, working with partners like EcoHydrate Solutions and Slow Food Kenya to ensure that we minimize environmental impacts and promote eco-consciousness.
